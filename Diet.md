CUTTING DIET PLAN

Goal: Fat loss to ~70 kg  
Calories: ~1600–1800 kcal  
Protein: High (~130–150g)

---

DAILY MEALS

Breakfast (8–9 AM)

- 50g oats
- 300 ml milk
- 1 banana (100–130g)
- 15g peanut butter

Pre-Workout

- 3–4 dates

Lunch / Post-Workout

- 3 eggs (minimal oil)
- 2 bread slices
- Light spread
- 50g whey protein

Dinner

Option 1 (Chicken Day)  
- 200g chicken breast (roasted/grilled)  
- Salad (cucumber/carrot)

Option 2 (Paneer Day)  
- 150g paneer (can go up to 200g)  
- 2 boiled eggs  
- Vegetables

---

WEEKLY STRUCTURE

- 4–5 days: Chicken
- 1–2 days: Paneer

---

DAILY HABITS

- Gym: 5x/week
- Steps: 7–8k/day
- Water: 2.5–3L

---

AVOID

- Extra peanut butter
- Extra cheese
- Junk / random snacking

---

PROGRESS TRACKING

- Weigh 3–4 times per week (morning, empty stomach)
- Target: ~0.5 kg weight loss per week

---

TARGET

- Expected timeline: 4–5 months to reach ~70 kg
- Stay consistent for visible results in 3–4 weeks
